# Changelog

All notable changes to Spark are documented here.

## 1.1.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-19

- Rebuilt the product screenshots as HTML and CSS components: inbox, playbook editor, channels, insights, and phone chat widget.
- Product views reflow to phone width, replacing the separate phone image in the hero.
- Added customer photos, channel badges, and a photo attachment to the product UI.
- Added integration logos to the agent's actions in the product UI.
- The product dropdown card shows the inbox conversation.
- The hero product window sits on a light grey canvas.
- Removed the phone mockup and floating status chips from the hero.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-07

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for SaaS products with home, pricing, about, careers, contact, industry, blog index, topic archive, post, search, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Hero over a faint dither with an announcement pill, a framed product screenshot, a phone mockup, and floating status chips.
- Animated playbook canvas with six use cases, ARIA tabs, and a pause button.
- Bento capabilities grid with live visuals.
- Pinned-screenshot platform section for Build, Launch, and Improve.
- Five product screenshots rendered at twice their display size.
- Count-up results, an orbiting integrations ring, and a scrolling customer logo strip.
- Feature story card and quote cards.
- Floating pill header with rounded dropdowns and a full-screen mobile menu.
- Pricing page with a billing toggle, a featured plan, and a sticky comparison table.
- About page with a timeline, leadership cards, and a team globe.
- Careers page with values, benefits, and filterable roles.
- Contact page with a topic picker, team inboxes, and offices.
- E-commerce industry page template with an intent table and a peak-week chart.
- Markdown and MDX blog with topics, co-authors, a table of contents, a call-to-action panel, a share sheet, and search.
- Photo blog covers from Pexels with credits, plus an icon-cover script.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Self-hosted Instrument Sans, Lucide icons, and indigo and lime design tokens.
